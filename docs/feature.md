EV Charging Management System (CMS) –
Feature Proposal

Investor Feature Report

Version: 1.0 (Pakistan Market)

Executive Summary

The proposed CMS is a cloud-based platform that enables businesses to manage EV charging
stations from a single dashboard. It supports OCPP 1.6J-compatible chargers from multiple
manufacturers and provides monitoring, billing, analytics, maintenance, security, and business
management features.

The platform is designed for:

●  Charging Network Operators (CPOs)
●  Fuel Stations
●  Shopping Malls
●  Hotels
●  Housing Societies
●  Corporate Offices
●  Fleet Operators
●  Government Organizations
●  Universities
